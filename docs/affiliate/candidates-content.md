5. **EXPERTE.com (Marvin Erdner / Janis von Bleichert)** — a commission-disclosed editorial comparison that ranks Nightwatch first among rank trackers; a scored, fact-checked review format, so inclusion carries more weight than a listicle.

## Notes

- The highest-leverage pattern is clear: five of these publishers already run a **rank-tracking** roundup whose top entries are Nightwatch, SE Ranking, Mangools, AccuRanker and Wincher. Those are the pages to pitch, because inclusion is an edit, not a new article.
- Several roundups now add an **AI-visibility** section (AI Overviews, ChatGPT/Gemini citations). Our rank tracking across Google, Maps and AI answer engines in one product is the differentiator to lead with — the incumbents on those lists mostly do one or the other.
- Of the competing programmes seen on these pages, published rates were Semrush up to 40% (12 months, then 10%), Mangools up to 35% lifetime, SE Ranking 30%, Surfer up to 25% on annual, WriterZen up to 30%. Our 35% recurring with no time cap, rising to 40% at ten concurrent referrals, is at the top of that range — worth stating as "no 12-month cliff".
- YouTube "About" business emails could not be read: the pages do not render for automated fetching. Those two contact routes need a manual check.
