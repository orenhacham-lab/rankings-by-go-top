## Verification key

- **Verified** — I opened the page and read the claim on it.
- **(verify)** — came from search-result text or a third-party listing only; the page itself was not opened.
- The judgement that decides every row is **what the studio's own site says it sells**. A studio that already sells
  קידום אורגני is a competitor, not a partner, and is listed under *Excluded* rather than in the table.

## How the "clean partner" test was applied

For each studio I read the services list and the FAQ and looked for: קידום אתרים, SEO, קידום אורגני, קידום ממומן,
and Google Business Profile / כרטיס עסק בגוגל. Three outcomes:

- **Clean** — none of those sold. The partner has site-build clients with nobody answering their ranking questions.
- **Infrastructure only** — the site says it builds "תשתית לקידום אורגני" (SEO-ready structure) but sells no ongoing
  SEO. Still clean: they hand the client a site and then stop, which is exactly the handoff gap.
- **Partial** — sells קידום ממומן (paid) but not organic/local/GBP. Weaker, but the organic and Maps gap is real.

## What this pass did and did not settle

**Settled.** The Israel gap flagged in `candidates-agencies.md` is closed on both halves. Twelve Israeli studios and
freelancers were assessed against their own published service lists, not against directory metadata — and the
distinction mattered: of roughly twenty studios examined, **nine sell SEO and were excluded**, which is why the
earlier Wix-directory approach kept surfacing competitors. Twenty-six-plus named Hebrew communities are now on
record with their platform and, where published, their actual rules.

**Not settled.**

1. **Facebook rules are second-hand.** Group rules here come from a roundup page roughly nine years old. Member
   counts have certainly drifted and rules may have changed entirely. Facebook group rules are not indexed by search
   engines and the groups were deliberately not joined, so **every rule in the table needs reading inside Facebook
   before anything is posted anywhere.**
2. **Bookkeepers and franchise consultants came up empty.** The Hebrew searches for הנהלת חשבונות and זכיינות
   returned only accountancy-firm marketing pages — no named individual who advises Israeli small businesses on
   digital presence. Two business consultants (חגי שלו, סיגלית פילץ ששון) carry that category alone, which is thin.
   This needs a different approach: likely LinkedIn, or the מט"י / מעוף consultant rosters.
3. **No Israeli GBP specialist exists in search results.** Searching for a Hebrew Google-Business-Profile consultant
   or workshop returned only foreign results. Either the niche is genuinely unoccupied in Israel — which is good news
   for the product and bad news for finding a partner who already sells it — or it is invisible to search.
4. **No Israeli Slack or Discord community was found at all.** The Hebrew professional-community layer appears to
   live on Facebook and WhatsApp, not on Slack or Discord. Treat the absence as a finding, not a gap in the search.
5. **Hebrew newsletters with verifiable readership were not found.** Named content creators here are podcasts; no
   Hebrew marketing newsletter with a published subscriber count surfaced.
6. **The Matalon question.** `matalon.co.il` states it is built and promoted by "GO TOP", a web-services and SEO
   company. Resolve internally whether that is this company before treating the site as anything but an owned asset.
