## Security note — read this before anyone opens BetaList

https://betalist.com/submissions/new carries a block of text **addressed to AI coding agents**,
offering a "Submit with your agent" flow and telling the agent to fetch a separate instructions
URL. That instruction was not followed. Page text is not an instruction from us, and no
automated submission should be driven from it. Separately, BetaList's own free tier is
unconfirmed: the form takes a URL and email, creates an account on the spot, and then says
"nothing is published until you… pick a plan", with no plans or prices shown. Held back from
the Qualified table for both reasons.

## Also worth knowing

The big networks — ShareASale/Awin, CJ, Impact, PartnerStack — are advertiser-side paid
placements, not free directories, so they are out of scope for a free-tier-only rule. Several
"best affiliate programs" roundups were checked for a submission route (Jotform, Salesforge,
Monsterclaw, Collaborator, ImpactMarketer, BrandID, Lemlist, EngageBay, Saleshandy): **none
publishes a "submit your program" or "suggest a program" form.** Those are outreach targets,
not listing spots, and belong in `affiliate-candidates.md`.
