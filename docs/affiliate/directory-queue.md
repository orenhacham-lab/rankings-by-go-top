# Affiliate directory queue

Places that list affiliate *programmes* for affiliates to find. One listing = a permanent
inbound channel for partners, unlike one-off outreach. Use the field pack below for each.

## Needs a real browser, unfinished

- **Affiliate.Watch** - /submit-affiliate-program is real and in the sitemap, but the site is JS-rendered and would not yield to fetching. Its sitemap shows forex, crypto and dating categories, which is an open brand risk to check before submitting.
- **SaaSAffiliate.com** - the best *fit* found anywhere (software-only directory whose SEO category already carries Semrush, Moz, SpyFu, SE Ranking) but it publishes no submission route; /for-affiliate-managers/ 404s.
- **BetaList** - held out: creates an account then says nothing publishes until you "pick a plan", with no plans shown. Its submission page also carries text addressed to AI agents pointing at a separate instructions URL, which was not followed.

## Lower value but clean

- **Uneed.best** - free waiting line, two required fields, no account to start. Lists the product, not the programme, so it is reach rather than partner discovery.
- **SaaSHub** - free, but needs an account and product-ownership verification, and has no affiliate-programme field.
- **SourceForge** - form loads and favicon-512.png fits, but it never states that listing is free and cross-sells paid packages. Confirm before submitting.

Note: **no "best affiliate programs" roundup accepts submissions.** Nine were checked. They are outreach targets, not listing targets.

Commission notes (271 chars, do not reword):
> 35% recurring, 40% from 10 active referrals. Full affiliate terms are in the Program terms
> field and at the affiliate program URL. These rates are the programme's current terms and
> we may change them for future referrals; a change never reduces commission already earned.

Standing rules for every listing: free tier only; the customer price range (79–649 USD) goes
in only where a field makes it mandatory, never converted to another currency, and never
without the tax sentence beside it in a public description; no URL in any free-text field
where the directory forbids links; no CAPTCHA solving, no payment, no uploads beyond a logo.

## Logo

Directories that ask for a square logo can use `public/favicon-512.png` (512x512) or
`public/gotop-primary.png` (500x500). Both are square and already in the repo, so there is
nothing to produce.
