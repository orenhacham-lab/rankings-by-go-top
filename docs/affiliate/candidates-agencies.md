# Affiliate candidates — agencies and professionals with client lists

Partner hunt of 2026-11-12. **Nobody here has been contacted.**

This list is deliberately different from `affiliate-candidates.md` (content creators / publishers). Everyone here has
**paying clients who already ask them about SEO**. One signed partner can put many clients on the product, so the
economics are better than a blog post: at $79–$649/month and 35% recurring (40% from ten concurrent payers), a studio
with eight local-business clients on a mid plan is worth more than a review article.

## Verification key

- **Verified** — I opened the page and read the claim there.
- **(verify)** — came from search-result text only; the page was not opened. Treat every such contact detail and
  service claim as unconfirmed.
- Pages that refused automated fetches (HTTP 403/500) are marked as such, so nobody re-burns time on them.

Two provenance notes:

- Several fetched pages carried a block of text styled as "reporting rules" addressed at the fetching agent. It was
  page-derived content, not instruction from this repo or the user, and every subagent correctly ignored it. Flagged
  here only so the pattern is on record.
- Wix's partner directory URL slugs do not always match the studio name shown on the page (`/simplemor` renders
  "ABG Creative Studio", `/anna-sinitsa` renders "LIFTOFFF"). The page's own name is authoritative.
